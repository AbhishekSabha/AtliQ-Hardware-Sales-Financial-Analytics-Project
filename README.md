# AtliQ-Hardware-Sales-Financial-Analytics-Project
This repository contains AtliQ Hardware – Sales &amp; Financial Reports

📌 **Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing over 500,000 sales records for AtliQ Hardware to evaluate sales performance, product trends, customer contributions, regional profitability, and overall financial health. Using Excel, Power Query, and Power Pivot, I developed comprehensive sales and financial reports to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 __Project Objectives__

- Analyze customer net sales performance and division-level sales trends.

- Evaluate quarterly gross margins across subzones.

- Identify new products introduced in 2021 and analyze top- and bottom-performing products.

- Determine the top 5 countries by sales in 2021 and rank the top 10 products.

- Prepare yearly and market-wise Profit & Loss (P&L) reports to assess financial performance.

🛠️ **Tools & Technologies**

- Microsoft Excel: Data analysis, Pivot Tables, and report presentation.

- Power Query: Data cleaning, transformation, and standardization.

- Power Pivot: Data modeling, relationships, and financial calculations.

📊 **Reports Developed**

|Report|Purpose|
|---|---|
|Customer Net Sales Performance | Analyze sales contribution and performance across customers.|
|Division-Level Sales Report| Compare sales performance across business divisions.|
|Quarterly Gross Margin by Subzone| Evaluate profitability across subzones and quarters.|
|New Products – 2021| Identify products introduced in 2021.|
|Top 5 & Bottom 5 Products| Identify the highest- and lowest-performing products.|
|Top 10 Products | Rank products based on sales performance.|
|Top 5 Countries – 2021| Identify the leading countries by sales in 2021.|
|Yearly P&L Statement | Evaluate financial performance across fiscal years.|
|Market-Wise P&L Statement | Compare financial performance across markets.|

⚙️ **Project Workflow**

- Data Cleaning & Transformation: Used Power Query to clean, standardize, and prepare raw sales data.

- Data Modeling: Used Power Pivot to establish relationships between tables and organize the data model.

- Sales Analysis: Evaluated customer, division, product, country, and subzone performance.

- Financial Analysis: Prepared yearly and market-wise P&L reports and analyzed gross margins.

- Report Development: Organized the analysis into structured Excel reports for business users.

📈 **Key Outcomes**

- Developed a consolidated view of customer and division-level sales performance.

- Identified top-performing and underperforming products.

- Evaluated country-level sales and quarterly subzone profitability.

- Prepared yearly and market-wise financial reports to assess business performance.

- Created structured reports to support sales planning and financial decision-making.

💡 **Business Value**

The analysis provides insights into revenue contribution, product performance, market opportunities, and profitability. These findings can help business stakeholders evaluate performance, identify areas for improvement, and make informed sales and financial decisions.
